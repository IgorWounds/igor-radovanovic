# Personal Website

This context describes the public-facing language for Igor Radovanovic's personal website. It keeps the site's identity terms precise so future edits preserve the intended narrative.

## Language

**Multidisciplinary Builder**:
The primary public identity for the site: a person whose work spans engineering, AI products, movement practice, and writing.
_Avoid_: Engineer-only profile, standard portfolio owner

**Current Work**:
The section for active professional commitments and products that are still being built or operated.
_Avoid_: Ventures

**Selected Work**:
The section for representative past projects and leadership examples, chosen for signal rather than exhaustiveness.
_Avoid_: Projects, portfolio grid

**Core Capabilities**:
The section for skills grouped by practical capability rather than a long keyword inventory.
_Avoid_: My Skills, technology dump

**Credentials**:
The section for patents, certifications, and third-party proof points.
_Avoid_: Achievements, trophy case

**Selected Writing**:
A professional supporting section for published writing, including _The Sigh of Silence_, without competing with the site's engineering and founder focus.
_Avoid_: Main CTA, oversized book showcase, theatrical framing
