About Me

I came into technology through data science and psychology. That combination still shapes how I work: I care about the model, the pipeline, and the person who has to make sense of the result.

As an engineer and lead, I like small teams with high trust, direct communication, and enough technical depth to make hard systems feel simple from the outside.

Outside work, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu keeps me honest about practice, feedback, and pressure. Books do something similar in a quieter way: they give me room to think before turning thought into work.
